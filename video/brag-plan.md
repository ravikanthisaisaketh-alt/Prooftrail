# Brag Plan: ProofTrail

Source of facts: `../pro_details.md` only. No numbers or claims beyond that file.

## What is this app?
A real, working skill-test and evidence platform that issues a verifiable receipt for every attempt; the learner decides who sees it.

## The angle
"A badge is not a skill. A receipt is evidence." Problem → receipt → live demo → how it works → honest numbers and limits.

## Hook
"Badges show you finished. Not that you can do the work."

## Tone
- Preset: polished (user direction: clean dark theme, one teal accent, large text, max 12 words per screen, fade + slide transitions)
- Duration: **120 s exactly** (user override of the default 15–25 s). Format: landscape 1920x1080, 30 fps.

## Visual identity
- Background #0b1115, surface #131c22, text #e8f1f2, muted #8fa4aa, single accent teal #2dd4bf (ProofTrail app uses teal #0f766e on light; brightened for dark).
- One family: Inter (headline 800, caption 500).

## Audio
Quiet music bed (bundled ende.app track, vol-1, 2:44 trimmed to 2:00). Volume 0.30 → dips to 0.10 during the 45 s demo so the user's own recording audio is not fought → back to 0.28 → fades out in the last 3 s. No SFX (restraint; dense text screens). No beat-locking or audio-reactive visuals: readability and fixed scene boundaries take priority (documented skip).
